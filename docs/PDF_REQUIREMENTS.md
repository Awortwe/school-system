## Policy/source decisions

- Montessori progress uses observation records (introduced, practising, independent, mastered) and narrative reporting. This follows the observation-based approach described by [Association Montessori Internationale](https://montessori-ami.org/questions/assessing-student-progress); the progress labels are editable application conventions, not a claim of one national Montessori grading standard.
- The assessment configuration form offers an editable 50/50 SBA/exam example, with missing scores incomplete, explicit exemptions excluded/reweighted, one-decimal rounding, optional competition/dense tie rankings, and manual reviewed progression. Schools must choose the appropriate level configuration. Proficiency-band terminology is sourced from [NaCCA curriculum guidance](https://nacca.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/SOCIAL-STUDIES.pdf).
- The payroll rule form contains a sourced, effective-dated example of the [GRA monthly PAYE schedule effective 1 September 2026](https://gra.gov.gh/domestic-tax/tax-types/paye/) and [SSNIT's 2026 insurable-earnings notice](https://www.ssnit.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Public-Notice-Min-Max-Insurable.pdf). It is not activated until saved by an authorised payroll approver. Employment coverage, reliefs and special payments must be verified by the school's accountant.

## Explicit exclusions and unfinished acceptance

Library, hostel, canteen, payment gateways, GPS and direct printer integrations remain excluded as the PDF requests. No actual school salaries, transport prices or depreciation rules were invented. Hardware print tests, actual mail delivery, off-site backup/restore acceptance, special payroll cases and production MySQL concurrency/load tests are not completed. Do not describe this installation as production-ready until those checks and school acceptance are complete.
